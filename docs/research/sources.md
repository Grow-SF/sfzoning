## Catalogs (noted, not accessed)

- **HathiTrust:** record 102439643, "Proposed comprehensive zoning plan" (1949 revision, UC copy, reportedly full view); record 003060870 and 000343514 (SF planning documents). catalog.hathitrust.org **blocked (403)**. Try in a browser.
- **Online Books Page:** lists a 1954 Dept. of City Planning "Comprehensive zoning ordinance" with HathiTrust page images.
- **SFPL / DigitalSF** (digitalsf.org/record/104561, 106817, 102128): municipal code volumes c.1960. **blocked (403)** to automated fetch; may need a browser, and possibly an SFPL login for some items.
- **SFPL San Francisco History Center:** the 1921 ordinance is call no. **917.946 Sa52b 1921** (from the owner's 2008 copy slip). SFPL Government Information Center holds most Planning Dept. reports above; archive.org copies are SFPL scans.
- **OAC:** Finding Aid to the San Francisco Department of City Planning Records (ark:/13030/c8z89jv6). Series 1 Subject Files 1922–2003. On-site only.
- **Pittsburgh City Archives:** holds a 1960 SF City Planning Code (physical).
- SF Planning internal: "A History of Low-Density Residential Districts in San Francisco," Passmore's "Abbreviated and Unofficial Timeline" (2017), McCutcheon's "Timeline of SF Planning History" (draft 2017). These are cited in the HAS white paper. **Not found online.**
