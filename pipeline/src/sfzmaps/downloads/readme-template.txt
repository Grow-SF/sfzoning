{{FIELDS}}HOW THE ZONING WAS ASSIGNED
The zoning maps were read from the scans, one district label per area, and
each label was assigned to today's lots. The boundaries in the lot and
district files are modern lot lines from the DataSF parcels layer. They were
never traced from the scan, so a district edge is only as precise as the lot it
falls on. Where a street has moved since the map was drawn, the scan keeps the
historic position and the data follows today's lot lines.

WHAT "confidence" MEANS
A number from 0 to 1 for how sure the reading is that the lot belongs to the
district shown. 1 means a person confirmed it. Low values mean the label was
hard to read, the lot straddles a district line, or the sheet was marginal.
0 means nothing could be read for the lot.

NOT AN OFFICIAL RECORD
This is a reconstruction, not an official record of the San Francisco
Planning Department. Check the Planning Department's own records before
relying on it for any legal purpose.
